## My Story

I’m Aileen Verdoza-Luevano, an MS in Business Analytics and Information Management (BAIM) student at Purdue’s Mitch Daniels School of Business. I did my undergrad at Purdue in Mathematics and Statistics, which gave me a strong foundation in probability and modeling—and eventually pulled me toward analytics because I like seeing math turn into an actual decision someone can use.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of predictive modeling, SQL/cloud pipelines, and clear communication. I’m comfortable tuning gradient-boosted models in Python, cleaning messy data in BigQuery, and explaining what a model's output actually means to a non-technical audience.

Next, I’m targeting full-time roles in data science, analytics, and operations research—especially in aviation, finance, retail, or tech where forecasting and quantitative modeling drive day-to-day operations.
